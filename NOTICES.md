Argiope's additional palette profiles are generated at development time with
the MIT-licensed [cusphanger](https://github.com/meodai/cusphanger),
[nutelch](https://github.com/meodai/nutelch), and
[Culori](https://github.com/Evercoder/culori) packages. These packages are not
runtime dependencies of the Neovim plugin.
